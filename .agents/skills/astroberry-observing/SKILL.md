name: astroberry-observing
description: Plan and supervise night-sky imaging with Astroberry, KStars and Ekos. Use for rig readiness, target framing, manual focus checks, finite capture sequences and review of saved exposures.
---

Read observatory_setup and query Ekos, mount and capture status. Treat the profile as configuration and check readiness against the current session. If the bridge is offline, plan from the operator's equipment details and identify checks that need a connection.

Confirm site and clock privately. Check the target fits the optical train's field, clears local obstructions and stays inside the configured altitude limits for the run. For the stock alt-az mount, verify controller alignment and tracking mode after alignment. Choose short unguided exposures and inspect for trailing and field rotation.

For manual focus, ask the operator to center a bright star and adjust the knob in small steps with the camera fitted. Compare the same star across short previews using apparent diameter or HFR when available. Recheck after changing optics or a temperature shift. If the preview shows only noise or broad gradients, establish star detection before proceeding.

Agree on the target, framing, exposure length, count, end time and finish actions before issuing control commands. Inspect an approved test frame for sharp stars, clipping, trailing and framing. Preserve any active queue. Follow the rig's commissioned capabilities for guiding, focusing and parking.

Monitor the finite sequence during the session. On completion or interruption, check saved files and report their location, actual count, summed exposure and observed quality. Verify agreed tracking and capture end states. If telemetry or files are unavailable, identify which outcomes remain unknown.
